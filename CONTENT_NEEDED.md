# Content Needed From The Greatest Concrete Solutions LLC

To keep the website truthful and high-converting, the business owner should supply:

## Verified project proof
- Before and after images for completed projects
- Project type, city, short scope and result description
- Materials or finish details, only when accurate
- Permission to publish each image

## Reviews
- Reviewer name or initials
- Review text
- Platform
- Public review URL
- Permission to display on the website

## Business details
- Business email, if public
- Operating hours
- Google Business Profile URL
- Social media URLs
- License, insurance, warranty, financing or guarantee details only if documentation supports public claims
- Owner or crew photo and approved short business story
